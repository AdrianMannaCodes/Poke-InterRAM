HAPPY 10TH BIRTHDAY,
SVETA!

A note, delivered by owl and grudging sincerity

Even Professor Snape is mildly impressed.

Ten years of existence, Sveta, and you have survived without a single exploded cauldron, which is more than can be said for most first years. Och, I suppose I ought to acknowledge it.

Do not let it go to your head. I shall expect you to be sharper in the coming year, and I shall be watching you with the same brooding disapproval I reserve for everyone else. Well done, nonetheless.

Yours, grudgingly,
S. Snape, Potions Master
